# Neuron shapes: credits (#78)

40 segments and drawn ~6× enlarged, and the cortical column of the zoom, #99, at true size) are real
reconstructions from **NeuroMorpho.Org**, licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
([terms](https://neuromorpho.org/useterm.jsp)). They are simplified (at most 300 segments a cell),
rotated and drawn as lines; these are changes to the original data. They are **illustrative**: the
simulated network uses point neurons (no dendrites are simulated), and **none of these cells is from the
person whose MRI the brain model is built from** — no data of that person exist at this scale.

## H01: real electron-microscopy reconstructions (#99)

The cortex close-up on `/brain` draws real cells from **H01**, a 1.4-petabyte electron-microscopy reconstruction of
about one cubic millimetre of human temporal cortex (a surgical sample), released by Google and the Lichtman lab:

> Shapson-Coe A, Januszewski M, Berger DR, ... Jain V, Lichtman JW (2024)
> A petavoxel fragment of human cerebral cortex reconstructed at nanoscale resolution. Science 384:eadk4858.
> doi:10.1126/science.adk4858

Data: `gs://h01-release` (https://h01-release.storage.googleapis.com). Licence, from its data page (read 2026-10-02):
"All released datasets are licensed under a
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)."

What is used (`web/neuron_render/build/fetch_h01.py`, the list of cell ids in `web/neuron_render/h01/cortex.json`):
- **proofread cells** (`data/20210601/proofread_104`): their multi-LOD meshes (decimated with quadric
  simplification to the triangle budgets of the phone and desktop sets; spines are the mesh's own), their SWC
  skeletons with H01's subcompartment classes (axon, dendrite, soma, AIS, myelinated axon), their synapses
  (`synapse_locations.csv`: the cell's presynaptic sites and its incoming synapses). The focus cell keeps its true
  position; the other cells keep their orientation and cortical depth and are moved sideways into the field (the 104
  proofread cells are spread over ~12 mm² of tissue); the manifest records every shift;
- **glia** (astrocytes, oligodendrocytes) from the automated c3 segmentation, picked from `c3/tables/somas.csv`: not
  proofread, labelled as such;
- a crop of the **EM image** (`4nm_raw` at its 64 nm mip, downsampled to 128 nm per pixel) through the focus cell's
  soma, drawn behind the block in the EM look.

Changes to the original data: decimation (the axon on its own share of the triangle budget), quantisation to 16 bits in each cell's box, lateral shifts, straight bridges (≤ 32 µm) across the gaps between the fragments of a cell's axon skeleton, the choice of
cells and levels of detail. None of these cells is from the person whose MRI the brain model is built from; the
activity drawn on them is simulated.

MICrONS (mouse visual cortex, CC BY 4.0) is not used yet: H01 covered every type the close-up draws.
