## kb1.jpg: LEFT hand on keyboard
- **Angle:** from behind and to the left, above the hand (over the left shoulder), looking down at about 45 to 50 degrees. The forearm in a dark sleeve comes in from the lower right. The hand fills about a third of the frame.
- **Fingers:** fingers held together and curled so the tips rest on the left letter block (index about R/F, middle about E/D, ring about W/S, little finger near A/Caps/Shift). Knuckles are raised and form a smooth dome. The thumb is tucked under the palm toward the left end of the space bar and is not visible. The heel of the hand hangs just past the front edge of the keyboard.
- **Edits:** keyboard maker's logo (top-left, beside Esc) and a small label under the monitor pixelated.

## kb2.jpg: LEFT hand on keyboard
- **Angle:** three-quarter view from behind-left and above (over the left shoulder), looking down at about 40 degrees. The whole desk is visible: a white tenkeyless keyboard, and the right hand on a mouse in the background.
- **Fingers:** relaxed and gently curved, slightly fanned. The index and middle fingertips rest around the R/T and E/D keys. The ring and little fingers curl down onto the left edge (S/A and Shift area). The thumb points down and to the right, resting near the left end of the space bar. The wrist is raised a little off the desk. The background also shows a right-hand mouse grip, with index and middle over the buttons and the thumb on the left side.
- **Edits:** a brand name on the wristband, a game URL/button on the screen, and a blurry out-of-focus object in the top-right corner pixelated.

## mouse1.jpg: RIGHT hand on mouse
- **Angle:** three-quarter view from behind and slightly left, above the hand (over the right shoulder), looking down at about 50 degrees. The hand is large in the frame. The sleeved forearm comes in from the lower left.
- **Fingers:** palm grip. The palm rests on the rear hump of a dark mouse. The index and middle fingers lie straight and close together along the left and right buttons, with tips at the front edge. The thumb lies along the near (left) side of the shell, curled slightly, tip toward the front. The ring and little fingers wrap the far (right) side and are hidden. The wrist rests on the desk.
- **Edits:** none needed. No logos are visible.

## mouse2.jpg: RIGHT hand on mouse
- **Angle:** straight top-down from behind (camera above the user's head), with fingers pointing up the frame. This is the clearest view of finger placement.
- **Fingers:** palm grip on a white wired mouse. The index finger is on the left button, left of the scroll wheel. The middle finger is on the right button, right of the wheel. Both tips sit at the front edge and the fingers are only slightly bent. The ring finger rests on the right edge of the shell and the little finger tucks under it along the right side. The thumb lies flat against the left side, tip at about mid-length. The palm covers the rear of the shell and the wrist is straight.
- **Edits:** a faint embossed brand mark on the keyboard pixelated.
